# :closed_book:【Book Recommendations (16 books for Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced levels)】

## (Beginner)

:open_book: **["Rich Dad, Poor Dad" by Robert T. Kiyosaki, Douban Score: 8.3](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E5%AF%8C%E7%88%B8%E7%88%B8%2C%E7%A9%B7%E7%88%B8%E7%88%B8%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

The world's best-selling financial management book. It has been translated into over 50 languages and sold tens of millions of copies. This book helps you intuitively understand the difference between the "rich mindset" and the "poor mindset," and helps build a basic understanding of cash flow and assets.

:open_book:**["Money, or The Dog" by Bodo Schäfer, Douban Score: 9.2](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E5%B0%8F%E7%8B%97%E9%92%B1%E9%92%B1%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

Europe's best-selling financial management book. It is simple and easy to understand, explaining how to manage money from a young age, rather than being controlled by it; how to think like the rich, correctly understand and use money; and how to invest and find the right way to accumulate wealth and achieve financial freedom early.

:open_book:**["Poor Charlie's Almanack" by Peter D. Kaufman, Douban Score: 8.6](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E7%A9%B7%E6%9F%A5%E7%90%86%E5%AE%9D%E5%85%B8%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

This book contains the complete biography and investment philosophy of Charlie Munger, as well as his major public speeches and media interviews over the past 20 years. It covers the investment evaluation process, an investment principles checklist, investment advice, and more.

:open_book:**["The Almanack of Naval Ravikant" by Eric Jorgenson, Douban Score: 8.4](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E7%BA%B3%E7%93%A6%E5%B0%94%E5%AE%9D%E5%85%B8%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

This book is a collection of Naval's content from Twitter, blogs, and interviews, published in 2022, with views that are highly relevant today. Making money is not about working hard, but about fully utilizing leverage.

:open_book:**["The Simple Way to Invest" by Qiu Guolu, Douban Score: 8.7](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E6%8A%95%E8%B5%84%E4%B8%AD%E6%9C%80%E7%AE%80%E5%8D%95%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%8B%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

A rare high-quality financial book written by a Chinese author. It proposes simple and actionable investment principles that ordinary people can understand, such as "cheap is the only hard truth" and "count the moon, not the stars." The author's views on stock selection, timing, and valuation are particularly in line with Buffett's value investing philosophy.

:open_book:**["The Intelligent Investor" by Benjamin Graham, Douban Score: 8.8](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E8%81%AA%E6%98%8E%E7%9A%84%E6%8A%95%E8%B5%84%E8%80%85%E3%80%8B(%E7%BE%8E)%E6%9C%AC%E6%9D%B0%E6%98%8E%C2%B7%E6%A0%BC%E9%9B%B7%E5%8E%84%E5%A7%86.pdf)**

A world-class and century-defining classic in the field of investment practice, often called the "Bible" of the stock market. The author, Benjamin Graham, is the "father of Wall Street" and Buffett's teacher. The book introduces portfolio strategies for both defensive and enterprising investors.

:open_book:**["The Index Fund Investment Guide" by "Bankeronwheels" (Xueqiu big V), Douban Score: 8.2](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E6%8C%87%E6%95%B0%E5%9F%BA%E9%87%91%E6%8A%95%E8%B5%84%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

Focuses on low-valuation index fund investing. It systematically explains various types of index funds and effective strategies for investing in them, teaching you "what to buy, how to buy; what to sell, how to sell."

## (Intermediate)

:open_book:**["The Most Important Thing" by Howard Marks, Douban Score: 8.7](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E6%8A%95%E8%B5%84%E6%9C%80%E9%87%8D%E8%A6%81%E7%9A%84%E4%BA%8B%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

Key concepts include "second-level thinking," the relationship between price and value, waiting patiently for opportunities, and diversified investing. It encourages investors to be "contrarian investors," wisely judge market cycles, and take bold yet cautious actions to achieve returns.

:open_book:**["Security Analysis" by Benjamin Graham, Douban Score: 9.0](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E8%AF%81%E5%88%B8%E5%88%86%E6%9E%90%E3%80%8B%E6%9C%AC%E6%9D%B0%E6%98%8E%E2%80%A2%E6%A0%BC%E9%9B%B7%E5%8E%84%E5%A7%86.pdf)**

This was the textbook Buffett used in his university courses. It systematically explains the practical methods of value investing and is a classic of classics, having been a bestseller for eighty years.

:open_book:**"The Advanced Way of Stock Market Investment" by Li Jie, Douban Score: 8.7**

This book is written for individual investors, drawing on the author's decades of experience. Its core content is divided into three parts, corresponding to the way of investment, the source of value, and the mystery of valuation.

:open_book:**["Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits" by Philip A. Fisher, Douban Score: 8.7](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E6%80%8E%E6%A0%B7%E9%80%89%E6%8B%A9%E6%88%90%E9%95%BF%E8%82%A1%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

The author, Philip A. Fisher, is known as the "father of growth stock investing" and is one of the most respected and admired investors on Wall Street. His investment philosophy has profoundly influenced investment masters like Buffett and Peter Lynch.

:open_book:**["Understanding Financial Statements in One Book" (new revised edition) by Xiao Xing, Douban Score: 8.9](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E4%B8%80%E6%9C%AC%E4%B9%A6%E8%AF%BB%E6%87%82%E8%B4%A2%E6%8A%A5%EF%BC%88%E5%85%A8%E6%96%B0%E4%BF%AE%E8%AE%A2%E7%89%88%EF%BC%89%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

This book is a classic work on interpreting and analyzing financial statements. It has been hailed by readers as "the best introductory book on finance I have ever read." The book starts by explaining how to read the three major financial statements—the balance sheet, the income statement, and the cash flow statement—and the relationships between them.

## (Advanced)

:open_book:**["The Art of Asset Allocation" by David M. Darst, Douban Score: 7.5](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E8%B5%84%E4%BA%A7%E9%85%8D%E7%BD%AE%E7%9A%84%E8%89%BA%E6%9C%AF(%E9%AB%98%E6%B8%85)%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

Darst, the founder of Morgan Stanley Investment Group, is known as the "number one person in asset allocation." The book systematically explains how to use modern asset allocation concepts to combine assets such as stocks, bonds, cash, gold, and real estate.

:open_book:**["The Crowd: A Study of the Popular Mind" by Gustave Le Bon, Douban Score: 8.8](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E4%B9%8C%E5%90%88%E4%B9%8B%E4%BC%97%E3%80%8B%E5%8F%A4%E6%96%AF%E5%A1%94%E5%A4%AB%C2%B7%E5%8B%92%E5%BA%9E.pdf)**

The most influential work in the field of social psychology, it exquisitely describes the collective mentality. Based on the psychological theory of the crowd, it is applied to the investment market to avoid excessive emotional fluctuations with market movements and prevent impulsive operations.

:open_book:**["Reminiscences of a Stock Operator" by Edwin Lefèvre, Douban Score: 8.8](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E8%82%A1%E7%A5%A8%E4%BD%9C%E6%89%8B%E5%9B%9E%E5%BF%86%E5%BD%95%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

"Reminiscences of a Stock Operator" is a designated stock market textbook by Buffett. It records the investment career of the legendary figure in the history of the US stock market: Jesse Livermore, known as the "Great Bear of Wall Street."

:open_book:**["Stocks for the Long Run" (5th Edition) by Jeremy Siegel, Douban Score: 8.6](https://github.com/codeman008/Financial_freedom/blob/main/%E3%80%8A%E8%82%A1%E5%B8%82%E9%95%BF%E7%BA%BF%E6%B3%95%E5%AE%9D%EF%BC%88%E5%8E%9F%E4%B9%A6%E7%AC%AC5%E7%89%88%EF%BC%89%E3%80%8B.pdf)**

"Stocks for the Long Run" is listed as one of the top ten best investment books of all time. Buffett once commented on this book: "This is an invaluable guide to stock market investing. Investors should seriously study Jeremy Siegel's new discoveries and ideas."
